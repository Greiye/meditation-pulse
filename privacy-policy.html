Privacy Policy

Pulse Counter · Effective 17 November 2026

Summary

Pulse Counter does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What the app does on your device

  • Vibration, sound and screen wake lock are used only while a timer runs, to pulse once per second and keep the screen on.
  • Offline cache: the app's own files (page, icons) are cached by your browser so it works without a connection. No personal information is in that cache.
  • Your timer settings stay in the app on your device and are never sent anywhere.

Data sent over the network

The only network traffic is downloading the app's own files from its web host (Cloudflare Pages). As with any website, the host may process standard technical data such as IP address and user agent to deliver and protect the service. We do not receive or use that data. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Permissions

The Android app requests no permissions beyond those needed to vibrate and display the app. It does not access location, contacts, camera, microphone, files or other apps.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
